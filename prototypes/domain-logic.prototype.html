Quizz prototype: Attempts and Certificates

The question: does the v1 model for Attempts and Certificates hold up in the awkward cases? It covers the deadline-based timer (an Attempt not submitted in time is Timed out when next accessed), the Retake Policy and cooldown, Assessment Versions, confirming the name, Revocation, Expiry, Learner Erasure, and Creator deletion versus Creator Ban. The rules come from .scratch/quizz-v1/spec.md. Everything is in memory with fake data. Click a scenario tab, or play freely.

Rules settled after the first round of this prototype:

  • There is no auto-submit. An Attempt not submitted by its deadline is Timed out: no score, can't pass, and it counts against the Retake Policy.
  • The Learner types their name when submitting. A pass issues the Certificate immediately; on a fail the name is thrown away.
  • A Learner holding a Valid Certificate for the Assessment can't start another Attempt. Once it has expired or been revoked, they can.
  • When a Certificate expires, the Learner's Attempt count for that Assessment starts again from zero, so they can always renew.
  • Assessment Statistics are running counters: Learner Erasure never lowers them. Name corrections are not counted as Revocations.

Deliberate choices this prototype makes. Check they feel right:

  • The Retake Policy counts every Attempt on the Assessment, across all Assessment Versions. The cooldown runs from when the Attempt ended; for a Timed-out Attempt, that's its deadline.
  • A name correction is started by the Learner: the old Certificate is revoked ("Name correction") and a new one is issued with a new ID.
  • The random question draw isn't modeled; each answer is just "correct" or "wrong".
